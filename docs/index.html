Set it, forget it, get in

Everything you would do by hand when a meeting is full — only faster, and without missing the moment a seat frees up.

🔁

Join until you're in

Opens the meeting in the Zoom app, closes the "meeting is full" dialog and tries again every few seconds (5 s minimum).

🔗

Any invite link

zoom.us and company hosts, web-client and webinar links with a registration token, or just the meeting ID. The passcode is used automatically.

🎤

Clicks through the preview

Turns your microphone and camera off on the preview screen, then presses Join for you.

🔔

Alarm when you're in

Beeps, brings Zoom to the front and shows a pop-up. The waiting room counts as a win: you have a slot.

🛡️

Guard mode

Keeps watching after you are in. Dropped out? It starts trying again on its own.

🖥️

Live console

A color banner, a meeting panel, a spinner while Zoom connects, a countdown between attempts and the attempt number in the window title.

How it works

No browser extension and no Zoom account changes — it drives the Zoom desktop app you already have.

Opens the meeting

The invite link becomes a zoommtg:// link that the Zoom app opens directly, with your passcode and name.

Reads Zoom's windows

Five times a second it checks Zoom's windows: full, connecting, preview, waiting room or in.

Clicks for you

On the preview screen it finds the mic, camera and Join buttons (UI Automation) and presses them.

Tries again

Full? The dialog is closed and the next attempt starts after the delay — until you are in or the time limit is reached.

Download Zoom War

Needs Windows 10 / 11, the Zoom desktop app and Python 3 (tick “Add to PATH”).

⬇ Download ZIP
1. Unzip it and double-click zoom_war.bat
2. Paste the Zoom invite link and choose your name
3. Leave the window open — you'll hear the alarm when you're in

# or from a terminal, with options:
python zoom_war.py "https://zoom.us/j/81234567890?pwd=xxxx" --name "Your Name" --delay 5 --limit 120

Questions

 

Does it work with the Zoom web client?

No — it needs the Zoom desktop app for Windows, which it opens through zoommtg:// links.

Will my mic or camera turn on by accident?

On the preview screen Zoom War turns both off before it presses Join. A checkbox that is already ticked is left alone, so it never switches your camera back on.

Is this spamming Zoom?

No. It waits at least 5 seconds between attempts and stops after the time limit (120 minutes by default). Use it only for meetings you are invited to.

Why does the window reopen when I double-click the .bat?

Windows Terminal ignores an app's own icon, so the launcher reopens in the classic console. That way the Zoom War icon shows in the title bar and on the taskbar.

Is it made by Zoom?

No. Zoom War is an independent, open-source project and is not affiliated with Zoom Communications.
